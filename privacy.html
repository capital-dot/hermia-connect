01

Who We Are

Hermia (ABN 75 434 177 026) is an Australian business providing AI-powered intake and lead qualification software. We help businesses automatically qualify, organise, and route existing enquiries using AI. We operate under the laws of Victoria, Australia.

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at capital@hermia.au.

02

Information We Process

Hermia processes three categories of information in the course of delivering the Service:

  • Client business information: Business name, contact name, email address, phone number, and billing information provided during onboarding and account management.
  • Customer enquiry information: Name, phone number, contact details, and enquiry data belonging to the Client's customers, passed to Hermia by the Client for processing through the Service.
  • Technical usage data: System logs, automation activity, and error reports generated in the course of operating the Service.
⚑ Important

Hermia does not source, harvest, generate, or purchase customer enquiry data. All customer information processed by Hermia is provided directly by the Client from their own existing enquiry channels. Hermia generally handles customer enquiry information on behalf of its Clients and only processes that information to provide the Service.

03

How Information Is Used

Information processed through the Service is used exclusively to:

  • Deliver and operate the Hermia intake and qualification service for the Client.
  • Route structured enquiry data into the Client's connected business management, CRM, or workflow platforms.
  • Process subscription payments securely through our payment provider.
  • Communicate with Clients regarding their account, service updates, and technical matters.
  • Improve the reliability and performance of our automation workflows.

Hermia does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

04

Third-Party Platforms

Hermia uses third-party platforms to deliver parts of the Service, including automation infrastructure, messaging delivery, payment processing, AI language processing, and CRM integrations. Each platform operates under its own privacy policy and data handling terms. The specific platforms in use may change over time as the Service evolves.

Where third-party platforms process data outside Australia — including in the United States or other jurisdictions where our service providers operate — Hermia takes reasonable steps to ensure those providers maintain appropriate data protection standards consistent with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Hermia is independent software and is not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with any third-party platform. All trademarks and platform names belong to their respective owners.

05

Client Responsibilities

The Client is the primary entity responsible for the lawful collection of customer enquiry information passed to Hermia. The Client is solely responsible for:

  • Ensuring all customers whose data is submitted to Hermia have provided appropriate consent in accordance with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth) and the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).
  • Ensuring any automated messaging sent on the Client's behalf complies with applicable telecommunications and anti-spam regulations.
  • Maintaining accurate and current records of customer consents obtained prior to engaging the Service.

Hermia processes customer data solely as a service provider acting on the Client's instructions and is not responsible for the Client's compliance with its own legal obligations.

06

Website & Cookies

The Hermia website may collect standard technical data when you visit, including IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, and session duration. This information is used solely to maintain and improve the website and is not linked to individual identities.

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies from payment and infrastructure providers, including session cookies required for secure payment processing. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or behavioural tracking technologies. You may configure your browser to refuse cookies, though some website functionality may be affected.

07

Security & Retention

Hermia uses commercially reasonable security measures to protect information processed through the Service, including encryption, access controls, and secure cloud infrastructure. Billing data is handled entirely by our payment processor and is not stored on Hermia systems.

We will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify customer enquiry data within 30 days of a Client's subscription ending, subject to backup retention schedules, legal obligations, and technical requirements. Client business information may be retained for longer where required by law or for legitimate accounting and record-keeping purposes.

08

Data Breaches

Where required under the Notifiable Data Breaches Scheme established under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), Hermia will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) of eligible data breaches as soon as practicable after becoming aware of them.

If you believe your personal information has been involved in a data breach, contact us immediately at capital@hermia.au.

09

Privacy Act Compliance

Hermia handles personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This includes collecting only information necessary for the Service, protecting information from misuse or unauthorised access, and not disclosing personal information to third parties except as required to deliver the Service or comply with law.

10

Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to request access to personal information we hold about you, and to request correction of information that is inaccurate, out of date, or incomplete. We may decline a request where permitted by law, and if we do, we will explain why in writing.

To make an access or correction request, contact us at capital@hermia.au. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with our obligations under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

You may also opt out of marketing communications at any time by contacting us or using the unsubscribe mechanism in any marketing email.

11

Privacy Complaints

How to raise a complaint

If you believe Hermia has breached the Australian Privacy Principles, you may submit a complaint to capital@hermia.au. Please include your name, contact details, and a description of your concern.

We will acknowledge your complaint within 5 business days and aim to provide a substantive response within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may escalate your complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au or by calling 1300 363 992.

12

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Service or applicable law. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date and become effective when published unless otherwise stated. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

13

Governance Contact

For all privacy-related enquiries or legal correspondence, contact us at capital@hermia.au.
